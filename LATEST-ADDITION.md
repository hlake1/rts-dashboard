### Narrative Arc

**THE GIRL WHO FINISHED LAST AT SEVEN, WON THE WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP AT FOURTEEN, AND THEN STARTED OVER**

1. **ADVERSITY (Origin):** Born in Corbera de Llobregat, near Barcelona. No motorsport family funding. Entered a sport with no established female championship. At seven years old, her mother encouraged her to enter the Catalan Junior Championship at their local village circuit. She finished eighth and last — dead last. She came back the following year.

2. **ACHIEVEMENT (Trial dominance):**
   - Age 12: Won the inaugural Women's Trial European Championship (competing against older, more experienced riders)
   - Age 14: Won the first official Women's Trial World Championship (2000)
   - **14 Women's Trial World Championships total**: 2000, 2001, 2002, 2003, 2004, 2005, 2006, 2008, 2009, 2010, 2011, 2012, 2013, 2021
   - **10 Women's Trial European Championships**
   - **5 Trial des Nations** (Spain team)
   - Most dominant record in her discipline by any rider, male or female, in a generation

3. **REINVENTION (The Dakar pivot):**
   - 2011: First Dakar Rally entry — an entirely different discipline (Trial = precision low-speed; Dakar = endurance, navigation, thousands of km of desert)
   - Finished 39th overall, won female class
   - 2012: Repeated 39th overall; only woman to finish the Dakar on a motorbike that year (rode solo after stage 4 hand injury)
   - **2015: 9th place overall at Dakar** — the best finish ever recorded by a female motorcycle rider in Dakar history. Not in a female category. 9th in the full field.
   - 2016: 15th overall (KTM)
   - 2020: 25th overall

4. **POST-DAKAR REINVENTION (Extreme E / W2RC):**
   - Joined Acciona | Sainz XE Team in Extreme E alongside Carlos Sainz Sr. (2021–2024)
   - 2023: 2nd in Extreme E Championship
   - Currently: FIA World Rally-Raid Championship with Gas Gas (2026), aged 40

**The Question:** What do you do when you've won your world fourteen times? You find another one.
